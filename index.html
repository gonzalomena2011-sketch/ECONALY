05 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Independent by design.

FINOVA is an independent student-led project exploring finance, economics, and technology. It is built around curiosity, careful research, transparent analysis, and practical digital tools.

It is educational and informational. It does not provide personalized financial advice, manage money, or recommend securities.
